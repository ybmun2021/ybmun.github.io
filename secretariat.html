Secretary-General

Ajay Jagath S K

Ajay Jagath, a Grade 12 student at Yuvabharathi, aspires to become an entrepreneur with purpose, someone who does not just build businesses, but creates meaningful change and impacts lives in ways that have never been imagined before. Ajay started MUNning in grade 9 and firmly believes that MUNs are not about awards, but about the knowledge and exposure one gains during the journey. He has been a key member of the YBMUN Design and Editing team since YBMUN III and served as the Under-Secretary-General for IT in YBMUN V. He served as the Prefect of the school (2025-26), and what sets him apart is his commitment to excellence, perseverance, and consistency in all his efforts.

In the field of STEM, he excels in coding and thrives on immediate problem-solving during Hackathons. He has received awards in notable STEM competitions. Additionally, he led an innovative development team that successfully created software for conducting the Student Council Election 2026 digitally. He also dedicates time to content creation, designing and video editing. He enthusiastically took on the role of Editor for the school newsletter, YB Mosaic - Vol. 4. He also participates in 10K and 5K marathons and in his free time, he enjoys playing piano and composing music.

 

Secretary-General

Mythili R K

Mythili, a Grade 12 student at Yuvabharathi, aspires to pursue management at the Indian Institute of Management (IIM) and build a career that combines leadership, innovation, and creativity. Her interest in Model United Nations began in the 7th grade, and to her, MUNs mean a great deal: the satisfaction of contributing meaningfully to discussions, defending her stance with reason, and working towards solutions through decorum and collaboration. From negotiating resolutions in committee rooms to organising events, she enjoys experiences that challenge her to think critically and work collaboratively. She bagged several Special Mentions and High Commendation in MUNs across international and national committees. Her leadership skills, rooted in accountability and hospitality, earned her the positions of Under-Secretary-General of Hospitality and Logistics in YBMUN IV, as well as Director-General and Vice Chair of ECOSOC in YBMUN V.

Beyond her interest in debate and diplomacy, Mythili pursues Carnatic music and archery - two disciplines that continue to shape her focus and perseverance. She excelled in the CBSE South Zone and National Archery in 2025. She has other diverse interests, including editing, designing, and running her own business. She firmly believes that MUNs have had, and will continue to have, a lasting impact on the way she thinks, communicates, and acts.
